Website and app privacy

This static website presents the app. The website, offline demo and banking edition handle different data, as described below.

Static website

This website has no banking login, credential forms, analytics or backend service of its own. Language selection and content playback take place in your browser. Do not enter banking information here.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the website and GitHub Releases distributes the APKs. GitHub may process connection data, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own policy. Support, bank and other service links lead to external destinations with their own terms.

Offline demo

The demo uses fictional identities, accounts and transactions and does not connect to the bank. Changes are stored locally. Resetting the demo or clearing app data removes that local state; uninstalling removes its private app data. Files exported elsewhere may remain at their destination.

Banking edition

The app sends requests to the bank when you use banking features. If you choose to remember access, credentials are encrypted with Android Keystore and require strong biometric authentication. Token enrollment requires explicit consent to the contract and encrypted seed storage. Sessions and generated codes are not stored persistently. Read the in-app policy for the full data handling details. SMS assistance is optional: Android requests approval to share one message, without SMS-inbox read permission. Only SMS-only login may auto-verify after that approval; keyboard autofill, combined questions and operations retain Verify.

Files and device services

Statement previews use a private cache. PDFs saved to a destination you choose may remain after logout or uninstallation and may sync under that provider’s settings. Sharing a PDF, CSV or QR gives the file to your chosen app; any copies it retains are governed by that app. External identity checks and Android or Google modules used for optional features have their own terms.

Limits and contact

This is an independent, experimental app. Bank authorization and owner-account validation remain unresolved; current compatibility has not been established. This work does not include a Google Play submission. Contact: bluegrain.lcc@gmail.com. Do not send credentials or personal banking information.
