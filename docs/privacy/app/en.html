Privacy policy

Santa Cruz Modern is an independent app, not official or endorsed by Banco Múltiple Santa Cruz. Responsible party: its independent developer. Privacy and support contact: bluegrain.lcc@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, codes or banking documents. Version: 2026-10-16.

Data and purpose. The app processes your input and bank responses: login, accounts, transactions, recipients, payments, cards, contracts and statements. It sends the bank the data needed for your requested action, security answers, this installation/device’s Android identifier, and device model/system information. The bank controls authentication, approval and outcomes and retains its records under its own policies.

SMS. The app does not read your SMS inbox or open message-sharing approval dialogs. Android’s autofill service or keyboard may suggest or fill the code depending on your phone and settings. Review or edit the code, then tap the verification button to send it to the bank. Autofill never submits the code by itself; manual entry is always available.

Storage and security. Sessions, account data and codes stay in memory. Remembering login is optional: username and password are encrypted with Android Keystore after biometric approval and require biometrics for use. Token BSC stores its seed, account/device binding and recovery phase encrypted after explicit consent. The app also retains language, terms acceptance, preferences and opaque markers for operations with uncertain outcomes. Bank communications use HTTPS with certificate validation. Remembering credentials starts selected on first login when strong biometrics are available; you can uncheck it. An encrypted username preview may appear before biometric approval without revealing the password. Saving credentials does not mean the bank accepted them.

Files and third parties. PDF previews are deleted on closure or the next launch after interruption. Temporary exports older than one day are deleted at the next launch or export. Files you save persist with your chosen provider, which may synchronize them. Sharing, printing or copying supplies data to the apps or clipboard you choose. Google Play Services provides scanning; ML Kit translates contracts on-device and downloads models. These SDKs may send technical data, installation identifiers, usage metrics and errors to Google. Contract text is not sent for translation. Identity verification, when required, occurs on the bank/provider website under their policies.

Retention and deletion. Logout removes the in-memory bank session but retains remembered login and Token BSC. Use Remove saved access to delete local credentials. Unlink Token BSC through its management screen after confirming the bank outcome. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local data; it does not cancel operations, delete bank records or remove saved documents. Do not clear pending markers without checking the outcome. Contact the bank about its records and accounts. This app does not create a separate developer account.

The developer does not sell data or display advertising. Receiving apps, Google and the bank have their own policies. Material changes will be reflected here and require renewed consent where applicable.
